# Faro — 1-minute video script

About 150 spoken words, around 60 seconds at a calm pace. English for Devpost and the judges; Spanish version below.

## English

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:08** | Black. A phone buzzes; the signal bars drop. | "In Latin America, when the ground shakes, the first thing to fail is the network. Right when you need your family most." |
| **0:08–0:15** | Faro logo, then the home screen: "All calm". | "This is Faro: a personal agent that keeps working when everything else fails." |
| **0:15–0:27** | "Live alerts" with real quakes in Peru, then the chat with Claude proposing actions. | "Online, it watches official quake, tsunami and flood alerts for Peru and Colombia, and Claude researches and acts for you: calendar, family check-ins, even your emergency go-bag." |
| **0:27–0:42** | Shake the phone → "Is it shaking?" → emergency mode with the radar. | "When the quake hits, it feels it. With no internet, it tells your family you're safe, finds water from your neighbors' agents over Bluetooth, and messages your trusted people, end-to-end encrypted." |
| **0:42–0:52** | Medical card, then a "Confirm" card. | "It knows your mom needs insulin and where your son is right now. And it never acts without your OK." |
| **0:52–1:00** | Faro logo and tagline. | "Faro. Your agent for the day everything fails, and for being ready every other day." |

## Español

> En Latinoamérica, cuando tiembla, lo primero que se cae es la señal. Justo cuando más necesitas a tu familia.
>
> Esto es Faro: un agente personal que sigue funcionando cuando todo falla.
>
> Con internet, vigila las alertas oficiales de sismos, tsunamis e inundaciones en Perú y Colombia, y Claude investiga y actúa por ti: tu agenda, los avisos a tu familia, hasta tu mochila de emergencia.
>
> Cuando tiembla, lo siente. Sin internet, avisa a tu familia que estás bien, consigue agua con los agentes de tus vecinos por Bluetooth y escribe a tus personas de confianza, cifrado de extremo a extremo.
>
> Sabe que tu mamá necesita insulina y dónde está tu hijo ahora mismo. Y nunca actúa sin tu OK.
>
> Faro. Tu agente para el día que todo falla, y para estar preparado todos los demás días.

## Recording tips

- **Record the real iPhone screen** (Control Center → Screen Recording) and lay the voice-over on top. Key shots: the quake alert, the radar, and a Claude card with "Confirm".
- **Film the "shake the phone" moment with another phone** so the hand is visible.
- **Check Claude answers with your API key before recording the chat.** It is the only part of the script not yet verified on the device. If it doesn't answer, change 0:15–0:27 to show only alerts and the go-bag.
